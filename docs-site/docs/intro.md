# Earth Network

Earth is a blockchain where **one human is one account**, proved with a passport
and a zero-knowledge proof, and where issuance is split evenly between people and
capital.

Most chains give power in proportion to how much you hold. Earth gives half of
its issuance to registered humans, one vote each, and half to capital. Neither
side can outvote the other, because they are separate streams.

## The two tokens

**ERTH** is the network's money. It pays fees, secures the chain through staking,
and is what the four emission streams pay out.

**ANML** is proof you are a registered human. Every registered person can claim
**1 ANML per day**, and nobody can claim twice, because registration is bound to
a passport rather than to a wallet.

## No allocation

There are no founder tokens, no team unlock and no investor tranche. Every ERTH
beyond the genesis pre-mine is earned — by registering, staking, providing
liquidity, or being voted a share of a fund.

The pre-mine itself went to liquidity, not to people. See
[Emission](./emission.md).
